## About Me

Hi, I'm **xuexiswmz**.

I'm on a journey from frontend development toward becoming a **one-person product team**. I'm interested in the entire process of turning an idea into a product—from design and development to launch and continuous iteration.

I enjoy exploring **Web technologies**, **interaction design**, and **AI applications** while continuously expanding what I can build on my own.

- 🌱 I'm working on my personal **“Zhao Hua Xi Shi” (朝花夕拾)** project—revisiting and reclaiming my technical knowledge in the age of AI, so that understanding, judgment, and implementation remain firmly in my own hands
- 🔨 I'm currently learning and practicing **code refactoring**
- 🧩 I care about the complete product journey: **idea → design → development → launch → analytics → iteration**
- 📝 I write about what I learn and build in my [personal blog repository](https://github.com/xuexiswmz/blog)
- 📫 Reach me at **2635003975@qq.com**
